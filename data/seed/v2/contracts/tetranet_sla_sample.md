## 1. Purpose and Precedence (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

1.1 This SAMPLE schedule sets out the service levels applicable to power operations at the Sites listed in Appendix A, which does not exist because this SAMPLE schedule is fiction.

1.2 Where this SAMPLE schedule conflicts with the master agreement it is said to sit under, the master agreement prevails, except on the measurement of service levels, where this schedule prevails.

1.3 "Outage" means a loss of service at a Site attributable to the power system, measured from the time the Operator's network management system records the loss and not from the time the Contractor is notified.

1.4 "Attendance" means the arrival of a competent person at the Site with the tools and spares reasonably required, and a person who arrives without them has not attended.

## 2. Service Levels (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

2.1 The Contractor shall acknowledge every Outage within fifteen (15) minutes of escalation, at any hour of any day, by recording an acknowledgement in the Operator's ticketing system.

2.2 The Contractor shall attend Site within the following SAMPLE times of escalation, measured to Attendance as defined in clause 1.4. These figures are invented for this SAMPLE.

| Site band (SAMPLE) | Attendance | Restoration |
|---|---|---|
| Urban, within 30 km of a regional store | 75 minutes | 180 minutes |
| Peri-urban, 30 to 90 km | 150 minutes | 300 minutes |
| Rural, beyond 90 km | 240 minutes | 480 minutes |

2.3 The Contractor shall restore service within the Restoration time in clause 2.2 on not less than eighty-eight per cent (88%) of Outages in each calendar month.

2.4 While an Outage remains open the Contractor shall record an update in the Operator's ticketing system at least every sixty (60) minutes, and a Site left without an update for more than one hundred and twenty (120) minutes is treated as unattended for the purposes of clause 4 (SAMPLE).

2.5 The Contractor shall maintain fuel at each generator-equipped Site sufficient for not less than seventy-two (72) hours of continuous running, and shall refuel within twelve (12) hours of a Site falling below forty per cent (40%) of tank capacity.

2.6 The Contractor shall maintain battery autonomy of not less than four (4) hours at each Site and shall replace any string measuring below eighty per cent (80%) of rated capacity within thirty (30) days of the measurement.

2.7 The Contractor shall achieve an availability of not less than ninety-nine point one per cent (99.1%) across the Sites in scope in each calendar month.

## 3. Escalation (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

3.1 The Contractor shall provide a single on-call number for each region, answered at every hour of every day, and shall notify the Operator in writing of any change not less than five (5) working days in advance.

3.2 Where an Outage is not acknowledged within clause 2.1, the Operator escalates to the Contractor's regional manager immediately, and to the Contractor's director of operations sixty (60) minutes later.

3.3 Where an Outage at a Site the Operator has designated critical is not restored within twice the Restoration time in clause 2.2, the Operator may escalate to the Contractor's director of operations without waiting for clause 3.2.

3.4 The Contractor shall attend a weekly operations review and a monthly performance review, and a failure to attend two consecutive monthly reviews is a material breach.

## 4. Service Credits (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

4.1 Where the Contractor fails to meet clause 2.3 in a calendar month, the Operator may claim a SAMPLE service credit of twenty-five per cent (25%) of the monthly charge for the affected region. The percentage is invented for this SAMPLE.

4.2 The credit in clause 4.1 is claimed once per calendar month in which the failure occurs and does not escalate with consecutive failures.

4.3 A separate SAMPLE credit of two per cent (2%) of the monthly charge for the affected region applies for each Site left unattended within the meaning of clause 2.4 for a continuous period exceeding six (6) hours, to an aggregate maximum of twenty per cent (20%).

4.4 Service credits under this clause 4 shall not in aggregate exceed thirty-five per cent (35%) of the monthly charge for the affected region in any calendar month.

4.5 A service credit shall be proposed by the Operator in the monthly report and shall not be applied to an invoice until the Contractor has had the dispute period in clause 6.2 to respond.

4.6 No service credit is payable in respect of an Outage for which a suspension under clause 5 (SAMPLE) has been validly recorded for the whole of the Restoration period.

## 5. Suspension of Service Levels (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

5.1 The service levels in clause 2 (SAMPLE) are suspended for any period during which the Contractor is prevented from working by a cause outside its reasonable control that it has recorded in the Operator's ticketing system at the time.

5.2 The following are the only causes on which the Contractor may rely: refusal or unavailability of Site access; a danger at or on the route to the Site; civil disturbance or a security incident; flooding or an impassable road; a public electricity supply failure where the Site is running on standby supply; a fault outside the power system; an outstanding third-party permit; and a maintenance window the Operator has approved in writing.

5.3 A suspension is valid only where its start time, end time and reason are recorded in the Operator's ticketing system, and a suspension recorded after the Outage is closed is not valid.

5.4 The Operator may reject a suspension within ten (10) working days of the end of the month, stating its reason.

5.5 Neither party may rely on a shortage of the Contractor's own staff, vehicles, fuel or spares as a cause under clause 5.1.

## 6. Measurement and Dispute (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

6.1 The Operator's ticketing system is the record of measurement for every service level in this SAMPLE schedule, and a time not recorded in it is not counted in the Contractor's favour.

6.2 The Contractor may dispute any line in the monthly report within ten (10) working days of receiving it; a line not disputed within that period is agreed.

6.3 A dispute shall be raised in writing, shall identify the Outage and the records relied upon, and shall be determined by the parties' operations managers within ten (10) working days of being raised.

6.4 Where the parties' operations managers do not agree, the dispute is referred to their commercial directors, and thereafter to a single mediator agreed between them.

6.5 The Operator shall not publish any performance figure concerning the Contractor to a third party other than a regulator without the Contractor's written consent.

## 7. Review of this Schedule (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

7.1 The parties shall review this SAMPLE schedule annually, and either party may propose a change to any figure in clause 2 (SAMPLE) at that review.

7.2 A change to a service level takes effect only from the first day of the calendar month following written agreement, and never retrospectively.

7.3 Where the Operator adds Sites to Appendix A, the service levels apply to those Sites from the first day of the calendar month following the addition.
